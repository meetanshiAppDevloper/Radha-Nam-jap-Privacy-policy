Privacy Policy for Radha Naam Jap

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Radha Naam Jap - Smart Counter, developed by MEETANSHI TECHNOLOGIES LLP. We are committed to protecting your privacy while you engage in your daily spiritual practice. This policy explains our data handling practices for this application.

2. Data Privacy & Local Storage

We respect the sanctity of your devotion. The app is designed to work primarily offline.

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or any personal identifiers.
  • No Registration: No account creation or login is required to use the app.
  • Local Storage: Your chanting counts, daily goals, and custom mantras are stored only on your device. We cannot access or recover this data.

3. Third-Party Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated technical data such as app launch frequency and crash reports. This data is non-identifiable and used only to improve app performance.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration (Haptics): To provide physical feedback during chanting.
  • Internet: Used only for Firebase Analytics and Google Fonts.
